# cindexdecomp: C-Index Decomposition for Survival Models

Decomposes concordance indices for survival models into Event-Event and
Event-Censored components to expose performance masking in highly
censored data. Supports Harrell's C, Uno's
inverse-probability-of-censoring-weighted C, time-truncated C, and
user-supplied pair weightings through a single engine.
